EQUIP Study

Equitable and inclusive research delivery and participation.

Developing an approach to help NHS research delivery teams support participation by racially minoritised communities.

In preparation · planned start January 2027

About EQUIP

EQUIP brings together the University of Staffordshire and NHS partners to explore barriers to inclusive trial delivery and co-design a practical response with the people involved in research delivery and participation.

The project includes a review of existing evidence, interviews and discussions with research delivery staff, followed by co-design work. Findings and resources will be shared here as the study progresses.

At a glance

Focus: inclusive clinical research delivery.
Setting: NHS and community participation.
Stage: project preparation.

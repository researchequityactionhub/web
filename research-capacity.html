Research capacity building

Giving nursing students a meaningful place in research.

Structured placements and undergraduate internships connect students with live projects, researchers and clinical research teams.

Nursing research placements

Research placements give undergraduate and MSc pre registration nursing students opportunities to see how research shapes practice and patient care. Students can contribute to live projects, learn from research teams and explore clinical academic careers.

Activities are matched to the project, the student's stage of learning and the time available. They may include reviewing literature, supporting approved research tasks, observing clinical research delivery and discussing how evidence informs care.

Learning through participation

The aim is for students to take part in purposeful work with supervision, reflection and a clear connection between academic learning and practice.

NIHR Undergraduate Internship Programme

Protected time to learn research in practice

In 2025, two undergraduate nursing interns each completed 300 hours of supervised research experience. They contributed to the FAIR Nursing Study and the Concept Ward Study, and gained exposure to clinical research at University Hospitals of North Midlands NHS Trust.

What the interns did

Work across live projects included literature reviewing, supporting data collection, project coordination and collaboration with research and clinical teams.

A flexible model

Both interns completed their hours over a summer period using schedules that accommodated their circumstances. The portfolio of projects allowed meaningful contributions within the time available.

Learning from this award supported two further completed NIHR undergraduate internships across nursing and healthcare sciences and helped five colleagues develop experience in supervising internships.

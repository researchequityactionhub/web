Studies / Black nursing and midwifery academics

Workforce mapping

Making Black nursing and midwifery academics visible in the data.

Mapping representation across UK higher education to inform more equitable academic careers and research capacity.

Analysis and reporting in progress

About this work

This study uses Freedom of Information responses from UK universities to examine the representation of Black nursing and midwifery academics, including access to roles with research responsibility.

It asks what the available workforce data can tell us, and where reporting gaps limit the evidence needed for institutional and sector action.

Why it matters

Clear, disaggregated workforce information can help institutions identify who is present, who progresses and who has access to research opportunities.
